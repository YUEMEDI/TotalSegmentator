Absolute agreement: ICC(2,1) for single-rater reliability and ICC(2,k) for average-rater reliability. In a random subset of 15 patients (all visits), intra-rater ICC(2,1) for keypoint coordinates was 0.93 (95% CI: 0.89--0.96), and inter-rater ICC(2,k) was 0.95 (95% CI: 0.92--0.97), indicating excellent agreement. Model-derived keypoints and Cobb angles were compared to manual annotations on repeat-annotated cases. The model's repeatability, assessed by re-running inference with different random seeds and Monte Carlo dropout, yielded a mean keypoint coordinate standard deviation of 1.8 px and Cobb angle standard deviation of 1.5$^\circ$, both substantially lower than inter-observer variability reported in the literature.
